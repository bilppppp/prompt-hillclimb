Validation effort must be proportional to the risk and size of the change.

Before adding a new test or validation mechanism, identify the concrete failure mode it is intended to prevent.

Once targeted validation has passed and there is no new evidence of additional risk, stop expanding validation.

Do not introduce hashes, snapshots, state files, reports, or other permanent machinery merely to prove that the task was completed.

Prefer the smallest implementation that fully satisfies the user's actual intent.
